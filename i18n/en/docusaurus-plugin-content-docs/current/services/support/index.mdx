# Support and Collaboration

Piksel provides comprehensive support through three main avenues:

1. **[Strategic Collaboration](./collaboration)** - Opportunities for collaboration in applying geospatial technologies and remote sensing, including data processing, analysis, platform development, and internship programmes.
2. **[Training Programme](./training)** - Practical training in satellite imagery and geospatial data discovery, processing, and analysis through Open Data Cube, Python, cloud-based workflows, and machine learning.
3. **[Help Centre](./help-center)** - An official channel for platform-related needs, including account access, bug reports, and feature suggestions.
