# Style and Inspect Data

Each layer in the Workbench has controls that help you read its data clearly.

## Open layer controls

Add a layer, then use its Workbench card. The primary controls provide **Ideal zoom**, **About data**, and **Show more actions**.

<Figure
  src="/img/maps/maps-style-01-workbench-card.webp"
  alt="Layer card controls"
  size={6}
/>

Click **Show more actions** to reveal additional settings.

<Figure
  src="/img/maps/maps-style-02-more-actions.webp"
  alt="Additional layer settings"
  size={6}
/>

## Change opacity and styles

- Drag **Opacity** to reveal or hide the basemap and other layers below.
- Use **Styles** to select an available band or visualisation for the layer.

<Figure
  src="/img/maps/maps-style-03-opacity.webp"
  alt="Layer opacity control"
  size={6}
/>

## Select time and use the timeline

Temporal layers show time controls on their Workbench card. Select another date or year with these controls. If needed, enable the timeline from the layer controls or **Map Settings** to show the time panel at the bottom.

<Figure
  src="/img/maps/maps-style-04-timeline.webp"
  alt="Temporal layer timeline"
  size={6}
/>

## Inspect feature information

Click a location on the map to open **Feature Information**. The panel shows information available from the layer at that location. Wait for it to finish loading before reading its values.

<Figure
  src="/img/maps/maps-style-05-feature-info.webp"
  alt="Feature Information panel"
  size={6}
/>

:::tip Combine settings
Try another style, adjust opacity, then click the map to understand how a layer represents the location you are analysing.
:::
