## About
Landsat 7 Surface Reflectance (Landsat 7) provides 30-metre surface reflectance data from the Enhanced Thematic Mapper Plus (ETM+) sensor, with an additional 15-metre panchromatic band for pan-sharpening. It is designed specifically for scientific analysis and time-series monitoring. The Landsat Ecosystem Disturbance Adaptive Processing System (LEDAPS) algorithm corrects for atmospheric scattering and absorption, enabling more accurate change detection.

:::note SLC-off since 31 May 2003
The ETM+ sensor's Scan Line Corrector (SLC) failed on 31 May 2003. Scenes acquired after that date contain diagonal data gaps, with about 22% of pixels missing from each scene. Data acquired before that date is unaffected and remains ideal for long-term time-series analysis.
:::

## Applications
- Development of derived products for monitoring land, inland waters and coastal features, including:
  - urban growth
  - coastal habitats
  - mining activities
  - agricultural activity (for example, livestock farming and irrigated or rainfed agriculture)
  - water extent
- Development of detailed information products, including:
  - measurements of detected surface-water extent
  - measurements of deforestation extent
  - crop-yield estimates
- Compliance surveys
- Emergency management
