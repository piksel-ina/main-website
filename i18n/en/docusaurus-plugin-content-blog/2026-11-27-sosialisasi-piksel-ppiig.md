The Geospatial Information Agency (BIG) continues to promote the use of geospatial technology through the development of the Piksel Platform. As part of its effort to expand collaboration with the academic community, BIG's Directorate of Geospatial Information Institutions and Networks held an online Piksel Application outreach session on Thursday, November 26, 2026. The session was attended by 42 participants representing the Geospatial Information Infrastructure Development Centers (PPIIG) at universities across Indonesia.

The session was opened by BIG's Deputy for Geospatial Information Infrastructure, Dr. Ibnu Sofian, M.Eng. In his remarks, he stressed the importance of using current technology to manage and process geospatial data, especially satellite imagery. Advances in computing and remote sensing give universities growing opportunities to conduct innovative research and produce geospatial information that benefits the public. In this context, Piksel is expected to become one of the tools supporting research and education in academia.

One notable session featured Prof. Bayu Taruna Widjaja, who shared his experience using the Piksel API for research and for commercializing precision agriculture technology. He noted that data access through Piksel is relatively faster than on some other platforms. His experience shows that cloud-based data processing infrastructure makes it easier for researchers to access and use remote sensing data for applied research.

In this session, participants also received an explanation of the Piksel Platform's technology, features, and capabilities, including the use of satellite imagery and the data processing facilities in Piksel Sandbox. The outreach session outlined how Piksel can support a wide range of research topics, the development of analysis methods, and technology-based learning in geospatial subjects at universities.

The participants' enthusiasm showed in the discussions and feedback throughout the session. One participant hoped the outreach would be followed up with technical training on using Piksel, so that the academic community can learn it in more depth and put it into practice. BIG welcomed this input as an opportunity to strengthen its partnership with universities.

Through this collaboration, Piksel is expected to be more than a platform that provides data access. It should also be an ecosystem that supports innovation, capacity building, and the advancement of geospatial research in Indonesia.
