Sentinel-2 data enables agricultural monitoring at 10-metre spatial resolution with a 5-day revisit cycle. This allows early detection of crop condition changes and identification of areas that need closer attention.

Through the Piksel Platform, this data can be accessed and processed directly in the Jupyter Sandbox — no manual downloads required.
