The Piksel Platform delivers a comprehensive solution for accessing and analysing satellite imagery across Indonesia. Built on Open Data Cube technology, it enables efficient and scalable geospatial data processing.

<!-- truncate -->

Through a strategic collaboration with Geoscience Australia, Piksel provides national geospatial data infrastructure that supports data-driven decision-making across sectors.
