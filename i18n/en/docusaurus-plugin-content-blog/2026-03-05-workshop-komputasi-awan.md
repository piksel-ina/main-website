Cloud computing is reshaping how we process geospatial data. Heavy investment in local hardware is no longer required — the Piksel Sandbox provides an integrated Jupyter environment with direct access to satellite imagery.

<!-- truncate -->

The workshop is designed for researchers, academics, and practitioners who want to apply cloud computing to geospatial analysis.
