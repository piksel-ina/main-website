As an archipelagic nation with high geological activity, Indonesia needs disaster response systems that are fast and accurate. Satellite imagery plays a critical role in providing real-time information on surface conditions.

<!-- truncate -->

Through Piksel, post-disaster data can be accessed quickly for damage assessment, identification of affected areas, and response planning.
