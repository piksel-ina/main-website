The northern coast of Java has undergone significant change over recent decades. Using time-series data from Landsat and Sentinel satellites, we can monitor and measure the rate of coastline change with high accuracy.

<!-- truncate -->

The Piksel Platform provides access to a historical archive that enables long-term analysis of Indonesia's coastal dynamics.
